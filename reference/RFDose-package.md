# RFDose: Deterministic and stochastic radiofrequency electromagnetic field (RF-EMF) dose calculator

Package for calculating RF-EMF doses for cohorts. Calculates the daily
RF-EMF dose of the brain and the whole body from the self-reported use
of wireless devices and the environment, with SAR values from the GOLIAT
dosimetric simulations. A stochastic version estimates the uncertainty
of the doses by drawing the parameters, SAR values and inputs from their
distributions.
